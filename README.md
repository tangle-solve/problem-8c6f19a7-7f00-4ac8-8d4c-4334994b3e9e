### CLIM-000264: Affordable multidecadal global storm-resolving ensembles
- **Subfield:** Climate-model inference and observing infrastructure
- **Open:** Produce coupled kilometer-scale ensembles long enough to estimate forced responses and extremes with closed physical budgets. Proposed moonshot: tenfold lower full-ensemble cost at matched validated skill.
- **Status:** ICON-Sapphire (2023) demonstrated integrated kilometer and subkilometer capabilities; sampling, spin-up, storage, and cost remain barriers.
- **Type:** moonshot
- **Why:** Enables direct scenario comparisons of organized storms and eddies.
- **Key works:** Hohenegger et al. (2023), [“ICON-Sapphire: simulating the components of the Earth system and their interactions at kilometer and subkilometer scales”](https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-16-779-2023).
- **Related:** Energy-efficient high-performance computing; rare-event ensemble allocation.
